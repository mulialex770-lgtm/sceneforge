# SceneForge Knowledge Engine

SceneForge includes a built-in creative knowledge layer that enriches Runway prompts. It is designed to make generated scenes more coherent and informative across common subject areas.

## Knowledge areas

- **Superheroes & comic-style worlds:** hero/villain goals, powers, silhouettes, action continuity, original-character safety.
- **Aliens & science fiction:** extraterrestrial biology, planets, spacecraft, gravity, atmosphere, speculative technology.
- **Humans & everyday life:** consistent identity, clothing, movement, social context and realistic physical behavior.
- **Money, business & economics:** cash flow, markets, prices, supply/demand, investment risk, entrepreneurship, inflation and currencies.
- **Cars & vehicles:** vehicle continuity, road geometry, wheels, acceleration, reflections, racing and electric vehicles.
- **Environment & ecology:** ecosystems, wildlife, water, soil, biodiversity, pollution and conservation.
- **Climate:** weather vs climate, heat, drought, flooding, storms, emissions, mitigation, adaptation and renewable energy.
- **Mythology & fantasy:** symbolic motifs and culturally grounded mythical beings.
- **Science & technology:** physics, chemistry, biology, space, robotics, AI and laboratory visuals.
- **Space & astronomy:** scale, starfields, zero-g cues, spacecraft, planetary curvature.
- **History & archaeology:** period-appropriate settings, clothing, architecture, tools and historical reconstruction.
- **Geography:** landscapes, cities, villages, regional architecture, vegetation and climate.
- **Animals & creatures:** realistic animal anatomy/behavior and consistent fantasy-creature rules.
- **Cinematic storytelling:** five-shot story structure, camera language, continuity and visual payoff.

The knowledge engine uses original prompt guidance. It does **not** contain a literal copy of all human knowledge, copyrighted comic scripts, movie footage, or proprietary image libraries.

## Five-shot structure

1. Establishing  
2. Character / Subject  
3. Action / Conflict  
4. Detail / Reaction  
5. Resolution / Payoff  

Each shot gets role-specific camera direction plus continuity and domain guidance.
